# Monaco race viewing — the full field of options

Researched 11 September 2026 for the 2027 Grand Prix. **Nothing here is contracted.** These are
published prices and ranges from the Automobile Club de Monaco's resellers and hospitality
operators, gathered so the catalogue can be organised properly rather than grown one option at a
time.

## The timing problem, stated plainly

**2027 prices are not published yet.** Sales open around September, which is now, so numbers will
firm up over the next few weeks. Everything below is either a 2026 price or a range a reseller is
currently quoting for 2027. Treat every figure as a band to re-check, not a price to publish — the
existing `why_price_note` pattern ("Researched from published prices, not contracted — we hold no
allocation") is the right disclosure and should stay on all of them.

One structural note that matters more than any single price: **the race moved to June.** Kyle's own
2026 footage is dated 4–7 June. Any option sourced against a May date is the wrong year.

## The field, cheapest first

### General admission

| Option                       | Published price | What you actually see                                       |
| ---------------------------- | --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Thursday general admission   | €30             | Track open, no F1 running. Practically a walk-around.       |
| Secteur Rocher — Sunday only | €200–500        | The Rock, standing, above the harbour section               |
| Secteur Rocher — three days  | ~€250           | Same, Friday through Sunday. **This is the budget answer.** |

The Rocher is the only general-admission ticket in Monaco and the single most important rung for an
Explorer tier. No seat, no shade, no allocated spot, and you go up at dawn on Sunday — all of which
our existing copy says, correctly.

### Grandstands

The ACM runs lettered tribunes. Current list seen across resellers: **A, B, K, L, N, O, P, T**.
X1 and X2 have been retired from the offer.

| Tribune        | Overlooks                                                           | Band                                        |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **K**          | Exit of Tabac, panoramic across the harbour                         | €190 (single day) – €1,290 (3-day / K-Gold) |
| **T**          | Swimming-pool complex chicane. Covered options; **sells out early** | €500–1,200+ Sunday                          |
| **B**          | Quai Albert Ier, along the harbour straight                         | €500–1,200+ Sunday                          |
| **L**          | Rascasse, the penultimate corner                                    | €200–500 Sunday                             |
| **A, N, O, P** | Positions not published consistently; mostly sold out by now        | —                                           |

Three-day grandstand packages generally land **€1,015–1,780**, with most seats at or above the
€1,000 mark.

**K-Gold** is worth noting as a distinct product: elevated seating within K, limited availability,
at the top of that band. It is a natural Classic→Premium step that we do not currently offer.

## Where the ladder has holes

Laying our four against the field, three gaps stand out:

1. **€890–3,000 yacht.** We jump from a $2,190 grandstand seat to a $6,380 yacht. The entry-level
   yacht market sits squarely in that gap and is the most obvious addition.
2. **Ermanno Friday (~£900).** A terrace with most of the circuit visible, for roughly the price of
   a good grandstand seat. Nothing we sell occupies that space.
3. **K-Gold.** An upgrade within a grandstand we already sell, which is the cheapest possible way to
   add a rung — same supplier, same relationship.

And one thing to verify rather than add: **Grandstand T sells out early** and is frequently gone by
now. If we ever move off K, T is the one to secure in advance.

## What to do with this

These are researched prices, not quotes. The next step is the outreach in the roadmap: pick the
three or four operators worth a relationship and ask them a specific question — ten people, these
dates, what is the rate. A published price is a starting point; a group rate is a product.
