# Supplier outreach — who to write to, and what to send

Drafted 11 September 2026. Three distinct asks, in the order they unblock things.

## The principle

**Do not ask "do you offer group discounts".** A revenue manager cannot price that question, so it
gets a brochure or nothing. Ask a specific number of people on a specific date and request a rate
sheet. That is a question with an answer, and the answer is a number.

Every template below is short on purpose. One paragraph of who we are, one of exactly what we want,
one ask. Nobody in a sales inbox reads more than that, and length reads as a weak position.

## The facts every email needs

Seeded departures, so these are the real dates and sizes:

| Trip                  | Dates                                 | Capacity |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------- | -------- |
| **Monaco Grand Prix** | 2–7 June 2027 (race weekend 4–6 June) | 50       |
| Southern France       | 14–22 May 2027                        | 14       |
| Southern France       | 11–19 June 2027                       | 14       |
| Southern France       | 17–25 September 2027                  | 12       |

Sender: `hello@guidelesstravel.com` · guidelesstravel.com · Guideless LLC

**Check the year and the month before sending.** The Monaco GP moved to June — a supplier quoting a
May date is quoting 2026, and we would be comparing against the wrong race.

# 1 — Trade partnership (do this first)

This one unblocks the others. As a trade partner of an already-authorised operator we can sell race
viewing legitimately and earn commission instead of paying a markup. Until it is settled, every
grandstand seat we sell is resale without authorisation.

**To:** `sales@grandprixgrandtours.com` · also +44 20 3966 5680 / WhatsApp 447458659680
**Also send to:** Senate Grand Prix (travel-agents contact), Gootickets (Monaco, ACM-authorised)

> **Subject: Travel trade partnership — Monaco 2027, group of up to 50**
>
> Hello,
>
> I run Guideless Travel (guidelesstravel.com), a small US operator selling organised, guide-free
> group trips. Our Monaco Grand Prix departure runs 2–7 June 2027 with capacity for 50 travellers,
> and we sell race viewing as a tiered choice — general admission through grandstand, terrace and
> yacht — so travellers pick their own level.
>
> I would like to set up a B2B agreement and get access to your pricing portal. Specifically:
>
> 1. Commission terms on ticket-only sales, not just full packages — we book our own hotels.
> 2. Whether allocation can be held against a departure before travellers have individually paid.
> 3. Grandstand K and T three-day availability for June 2027, and whether K-Gold is available to the
>    trade.
>
> We are a US seller, so your ATOL cover will not apply to us — I mention it only so the agreement
> is not drafted around it.
>
> Happy to send our company details and references.

**Why these three questions.** Ticket-only matters because we already have hotel supply and do not
want a package we cannot unbundle. Allocation-before-payment is the one that decides whether we can
sell a tier honestly — right now our copy says "we hold no allocation", and that is a real
limitation we should either fix or keep disclosing. K-Gold is the cheapest rung we could add.

# 2 — Direct suppliers (group rates)

Send these in parallel. They take days to come back, so the clock matters more than the polish.

## Yacht hospitality — Port Hercule

The entry-level yacht market runs €890–€3,000 and we sell nothing in it. Approach the operators
below that tier, not only Amber Lounge.

**To:** Amber Lounge, and the Port Hercule yacht hospitality operators (Senate, Red Eye Events,
Vision4Sports, Rocket Hospitality all broker the same berths)

> **Subject: 10 guests, Monaco race weekend 2027 — rate for a group**
>
> Hello,
>
> Guideless Travel runs a Monaco Grand Prix departure from 2–7 June 2027. I am looking at yacht
> hospitality for **ten guests**, and I would rather book one block than ten individual places.
>
> Could you send me a rate sheet for:
>
> - Ten guests, qualifying day (Saturday 5 June)
> - Ten guests, race day (Sunday 6 June)
> - Ten guests, both days
>
> I am specifically asking what ten together does to the per-head price against your published rate,
> and whether you hold the places once booked. If you have availability at more than one price
> point — a smaller boat as well as the flagship — I would like both, because our travellers choose
> their own level.

## Grandstands — direct

Worth sending even with the trade partnership in flight; they price differently.

**To:** ACM ticketing (`billetterie@acm.mc` via acm.mc), Gootickets

> **Subject: Block booking, Monaco 2027 — 12 to 50 seats**
>
> Hello,
>
> Guideless Travel runs a group departure for the 2027 Grand Prix, 2–7 June, with up to 50
> travellers. Not all of them will choose a grandstand, so I am asking about a block of **12 to 50
> three-day seats**, most likely Tribune K or T.
>
> Three questions:
>
> 1. Is a block held against a group booking, and what deposit holds it?
> 2. Is there a trade or group rate against the published price?
> 3. What is the last date a block can be released without penalty?
>
> We tell our travellers plainly when we hold an allocation and when we do not, so a clear answer to
> the first question is worth more to me than a discount.

**To:** the four linked properties, plus the Beausoleil and Nice candidates from the tier research

>

>

## Rail — SNCF groups

For the Nice–Avignon–Paris legs, 6–14 travellers per departure.

> **Subject: Group rail, Nice–Avignon–Paris, three 2027 departures**
>
> Hello,
>
> Guideless Travel runs Southern France departures on 14–22 May, 11–19 June and 17–25 September
> 2027, each with **6 to 14 travellers**, all needing Nice → Avignon → Paris.
>
> Could you tell me what a group booking of that size gets against standard fares, and how far ahead
> it can be confirmed?

# 3 — Omio Booking API

Rail without a relationship per operator. Ask for the Booking API rather than Meta Search: Meta
Search hands the traveller to Omio's checkout, which contradicts telling people we book every part
of the trip.

**To:** Omio B2B (omio.com/b2b)

> **Subject: Booking API access — small operator, European rail**
>
> Hello,
>
> Guideless Travel (guidelesstravel.com) is a small US operator running organised group trips in
> Europe. Our itineraries include rail between French cities — Nice, Avignon and Paris — and we book
> every leg on the traveller's behalf rather than handing them off to book it themselves.
>
> That means the **Booking API** rather than Meta Search: we need to keep payment and the booking
> inside our own journey. Could you tell me:
>
> 1. What the commercial terms and minimum volumes are for an operator of our size.
> 2. Whether French domestic rail (SNCF) is covered under that model.
> 3. Whether Rail Europe's B2B terms change anything now that the acquisition is under way.
>
> Volumes today are small — three departures in 2027 of 6–14 travellers each — and I would rather
> say so than overstate them.

## Tracking the replies

There is nowhere in the product to record any of this. Until there is, a spreadsheet with supplier,
date sent, who replied, the rate quoted and whether allocation is held will do — but the answers
belong in the catalogue eventually, because `why_price_note` currently says "researched, not
contracted" on every option and each signed rate is one fewer place that is true.
