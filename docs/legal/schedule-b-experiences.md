## B1. What the Operator provides

B1.1 The Operator provides the activity described in `[DESCRIPTION REFERENCE]`, including
`[WHAT IS INCLUDED]`.

B1.2 Not included, and the traveler pays or brings: `[WHAT IS NOT INCLUDED]`.

B1.3 Guideless publishes this description to travelers before they buy. **The activity delivered
must match it.** If the Operator changes the route, the vessel, the venue, the duration or what is
included, it must tell Guideless in writing before travelers are affected.

B1.4 **No Guideless representative attends.** The Operator receives travelers directly, checks them
against the name list, briefs them, runs the activity and accounts for everyone at the end. Where an
activity needs a head count or a supervising adult, that is the Operator's responsibility.

> _Note: B1.4 is the defining feature of the product. The Operator is the only professional present,
> which is why the safety and reporting duties below sit entirely with it._

B2.4 Staff-to-guest ratio: `[RATIO]`, and never worse than any legal or licensing minimum.

B2.5 Guideless travelers `[SHARE / DO NOT SHARE]` the activity with other customers. Where they

## B3. Bookings, late additions and names

B3.1 Guideless books under clause 6 of the master agreement.

B3.2 **Travelers may buy this activity during the trip**, sometimes on the day. The Operator will
accept a late addition up to `[LATE BOOKING CUT-OFF]` before the start, subject to capacity, at the
contracted rate.

B3.3 Guideless provides a name list `[NAME LIST DAYS]` days before, and an updated list if it
changes. The Operator will accept a name change up to `[NAME CHANGE CUT-OFF]` before the start.

B3.4 The Operator will not require a traveler to create an account, sign up to marketing, or accept
the Operator's own terms as a condition of taking part. Clause 22 of the master agreement applies.

## B4. Safety

B4.1 The Operator holds a current written risk assessment for this activity and follows its own
safety procedures.

B4.2 **The Operator gives a safety briefing before every departure**, in `[LANGUAGES]`, covering at
least: what the activity involves, the hazards, what travelers must and must not do, what to do in
an emergency, where the safety equipment is, and how to signal for help.

B4.3 The Operator provides all safety equipment the activity requires, in serviceable condition and
in sizes to fit every traveler, including `[EQUIPMENT LIST]`. Equipment is included in the rate and
may not be charged separately.

B4.4 The Operator will refuse participation, without charge to Guideless beyond the normal
cancellation terms, where a traveler is unfit to take part safely, is intoxicated, or refuses to
follow the briefing. It will tell Guideless the same day.

B4.5 The Operator will report incidents under clause 14 of the master agreement: **immediately, at
any hour**, to `[24/7 PHONE NUMBER]`.

## B5. Weather, sea state and conditions

B5.1 The Operator decides whether conditions are safe. That decision is the Operator's alone and
Guideless will not question it.

B5.2 The Operator will decide as early as it reasonably can, and will tell Guideless by
`[WEATHER DECISION TIME]` on the day at the latest, so travelers are not left waiting.

B5.3 If the Operator cancels for weather or conditions, Guideless is **refunded in full** within
`[REFUND DAYS]` days. The Operator may instead offer an alternative date within the traveler's trip,
which Guideless may accept or decline.

B5.4 If the Operator shortens or alters the activity materially because of conditions — a shorter
route, no swim stop, a different landing — it will refund the proportion of the rate that reflects
what was not delivered, agreed with Guideless in good faith.

B5.5 A cancellation for weather is not force majeure under clause 12 of the master agreement. It is
governed by this clause, and money is returned in full.

> _Note: B5 is written for the Nice-to-Monaco coastal boat, where sea state genuinely does cancel
> departures. B5.5 avoids the argument that weather is force majeure and therefore that the Operator
> may keep costs — Guideless must refund its traveler, so it must be made whole._

## B6. Age, health and access

B6.1 Minimum age: `[MINIMUM AGE]`. Maximum age, if any: `[MAXIMUM AGE]`.

B6.2 A traveler under `[SUPERVISION AGE]` must be accompanied by an adult on the same booking.

B6.3 Health, fitness or other restrictions that would stop someone taking part:
`[RESTRICTIONS — e.g. pregnancy, back or neck conditions, heart conditions, non-swimmers, mobility]`.

B6.4 The Operator states these restrictions here so Guideless can publish them **before** a traveler
buys. A restriction the Operator applies on the day but did not disclose here is the Operator's
problem: Guideless is refunded in full and the Operator meets any resulting cost.

B6.5 Where the activity can accommodate a traveler with reduced mobility or another disability, the
Operator states how here: `[ACCESSIBILITY]`. Where it cannot, it says so plainly rather than
deciding at the quayside.

> _Note: B6.4 is the clause that prevents the worst version of this failing — a traveler who paid,
> travelled, and is turned away at the start for a reason nobody told them._

## B7. Late arrival and no-shows

B7.1 The Operator will wait `[GRACE MINUTES]` minutes past the stated start for a late traveler
where doing so does not affect the activity or other guests.

B7.2 A traveler who does not arrive is a no-show and is charged in full. The Operator will tell
Guideless the same day so Guideless can find out whether something has happened to them.

B7.3 The Operator will not leave a traveler behind at an intermediate stop without contacting
Guideless immediately and making a reasonable effort to reunite them with the group.

> _Note: B7.2's reporting duty is not commercial. With no Guideless representative present, a
> no-show is the first signal that a traveler may be ill, lost or in trouble._

## B8. Alcohol

B8.1 Where the activity includes alcohol — a tasting, a dinner, an open bar — the Operator serves it
lawfully and responsibly, and will refuse service to anyone under the legal age or visibly
intoxicated.

B8.2 The Operator will always offer a non-alcoholic alternative at no extra cost.

B8.3 The Operator will not make alcohol the condition of taking part, and will not press a traveler
who declines.

B8.4 Where the activity is a tasting, the quantities are `[TASTING QUANTITIES]` and the Operator
will make water and food available throughout.

## B9. Photography and traveler images

B9.1 The Operator may take photographs or video during the activity **only** where it has told
travelers at the briefing and given them a practical way to decline.

B9.2 The Operator may not publish, sell or use a traveler's image in its own marketing without that
traveler's specific consent, recorded at the time.

B9.3 A traveler who declines must still receive the full activity. Consent may not be a condition of
taking part.

B9.4 Where the Operator sells photographs to travelers, that is between the Operator and the
traveler and is not part of the rate.

> _Note: B9 exists because a traveler's photograph is personal data under the GDPR and the group is
> in the EU while the activity happens. Consent must be freely given, which is why B9.3 is there._
