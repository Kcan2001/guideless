D1.2 **Monaco grandstand tickets are normally sold as a three-day pass covering Friday, Saturday and
Sunday.** Where a product covers fewer sessions, D1.1 must say so exactly. Guideless publishes the
sessions to travelers and prices on them, so a mismatch is a mis-sale.

D1.3 Where the product is hospitality, D1.1 is supplemented by a written description of what is
included — food, drink, staffing, screens, access times, and whether a race view is guaranteed from
a seat or is standing. That description is attached as `[HOSPITALITY DESCRIPTION REFERENCE]`.

D1.4 The Supplier warrants that it is entitled to sell these tickets, that they are genuine, and
that they will admit the holder to the sessions stated.

> _Note: D1.2 exists because Guideless's own catalogue previously described a Monaco grandstand as a
> Saturday-and-Sunday product, which is not how the event sells. Getting this wrong means selling
> something that does not exist._

## D4. Non-refundable, and what that actually means

D4.1 Guideless accepts that once tickets are **issued** they are normally non-refundable and
non-transferable, and that this is standard for this event.

D4.2 The Supplier states the point of no return here: tickets are treated as issued on
`[ISSUE DATE OR TRIGGER]`. **Before that date the cancellation terms in D5 apply.** The Supplier may
not treat a ticket as issued earlier than this in order to refuse a refund.

D4.3 The Supplier will tell Guideless in writing when tickets are issued.

D4.4 Non-refundable does not mean non-replaceable. If a ticket is lost, damaged, or fails to admit
the holder through no fault of the traveler, the Supplier will replace it or provide equivalent
access at its own cost.

## D7. Delivery

D7.1 The Supplier delivers tickets by `[DELIVERY METHOD — e-ticket / collection / courier]` no later
than `[DELIVERY DEADLINE]`.

D7.2 **Guideless travelers arrive from overseas and are not at a home address in the days before the

D7.3 Where collection in person is required, the Supplier states exactly where, when, what
identification is needed, and whether the lead traveler may collect for the whole party:
`[COLLECTION ARRANGEMENT]`.

D7.4 If tickets have not been delivered `[ESCALATION DAYS]` days before the first session, the
Supplier will escalate to `[SUPPLIER ESCALATION CONTACT]` and tell Guideless the same day.

D7.5 Failure to deliver a ticket in time for the traveler to use it is a failure to perform. Clause
10.3 of the master agreement applies, and the Supplier refunds in full and meets Guideless's
reasonable direct costs.

> _Note: D7.2 is a practical failure mode rather than a legal nicety. Monaco tickets are often
> released close to the event, and a courier to a US address the week the traveler is already in
> France is a guaranteed problem._

## D8. If the event is cancelled, shortened or run without spectators

D8.1 **Event cancelled entirely.** The Supplier refunds everything Guideless has paid within
`[REFUND DAYS]` days, less only what the event organiser irrecoverably retains and the Supplier can
evidence. Where the organiser refunds the Supplier, the Supplier passes that through in full.

D8.2 **A session is cancelled or shortened** — a race stopped early, a practice abandoned,
qualifying postponed. The Supplier passes through whatever the organiser gives, and states here what
its own policy is where the organiser gives nothing: `[SHORTENED SESSION POLICY]`.

D8.3 **Run without spectators.** Treated as a cancellation under D8.1.

D8.4 **Access denied on the day** for a reason within the Supplier's or the venue's control — a
closed stand, an overbooked terrace, a withdrawn pass — is a failure to perform, not an event
cancellation. The Supplier provides equivalent or better access at its own cost, or refunds in full
and meets Guideless's reasonable direct costs.

D8.5 The Supplier will tell Guideless immediately of any organiser announcement affecting access,
timing or format, at any hour, under clause 14.4 of the master agreement.

> _Note: D8.1 to D8.3 need checking against whatever the Automobile Club de Monaco's own terms say,
> because the Supplier can only pass through what it receives. D8.4 is different in kind and should
> not be allowed to shelter under the same heading._

## D9. If the date moves

D9.1 The parties record that the 2027 Formula 1 calendar is **subject to approval and change by the
FIA World Motor Sport Council**, and that the Monaco dates in this schedule are as currently
published rather than finally fixed.

D9.2 If the event moves to different dates, the Supplier will transfer Guideless's allocation to the
new dates at no charge.

D9.3 Guideless builds a whole trip around the event — hotels, rail, transfers and activities booked
against these dates. If the new dates make the trip unworkable, **Guideless may cancel and receive a
full refund** of everything paid under this schedule, provided it cancels within
`[DATE CHANGE DECISION DAYS]` days of the change being announced.

D9.4 The Supplier will notify Guideless of any announced or proposed date change within
`[DATE CHANGE NOTIFICATION DAYS]` days of learning of it, whether or not it is confirmed.

> _Note: D9 is the clause most likely to be resisted and most worth keeping. A date move that
> Guideless cannot cancel out of leaves it holding non-refundable tickets for a weekend on which it
> has no hotel rooms._

## D10. Resale and use

D10.1 Guideless sells these tickets only as part of a Guideless trip, to travelers on that trip. It
will not resell them on the open market or to ticket resellers.

D10.2 The Supplier will not impose resale restrictions on Guideless beyond those the event organiser
imposes, and will state those here: `[ORGANISER RESTRICTIONS]`.

D10.3 The Supplier confirms that selling the ticket as part of a package trip is permitted by the
organiser's terms. If it is not, it must say so before Guideless sells anything.

D10.4 The Supplier will provide the organiser's conditions of entry — prohibited items, bag rules,
age limits, accessibility — in time for Guideless to publish them to travelers before they buy:
`[CONDITIONS OF ENTRY REFERENCE]`.

> _Note: D10.3 is worth an explicit answer in writing. Some event organisers restrict the packaging
> of tickets with accommodation to appointed operators, and buying from a broker does not cure that._
