## C1. The service

C1.1 The Operator provides private point-to-point transfers for Guideless travelers on the routes
and at the rates in C2.

C1.2 A transfer is **private to one booking**. The Operator will not add other passengers unless
Guideless has booked a shared transfer and said so.

C1.3 Travelers book their own flights and arrive independently, at any hour and often on different
days from each other. There is no Guideless representative at the airport. **The Operator meets the
traveler itself.**

C1.4 The price Guideless pays is per vehicle, not per passenger, unless C2 says otherwise.

> _Note: C1.3 is the operating reality. Guideless sells the transfer as an optional extra per
> booking, so the Operator is meeting one or two people off an unpredictable flight, not a coach
> load off a charter._

## C3. Licensing, vehicles and drivers

C3.1 The Operator holds every licence its business requires, including
`[LICENCES — e.g. French VTC / capacité de transport, Monaco authorisation]`, and will provide copies
on request.

C3.2 Vehicles are licensed for passenger hire, insured for it, no older than `[VEHICLE AGE]` years,
and hold a current roadworthiness certificate.

C3.3 Every driver holds a current licence valid for passenger hire, and the Operator has checked it.
The Operator will re-check at least annually.

C3.4 The Operator carries out background checks on drivers to the extent the law allows, and will
not use a driver it would not put with an unaccompanied traveler.

C3.5 Vehicles carry a working seat belt for every passenger, air conditioning, and a phone charger
where the class in C2 says so.

C3.6 Child seats: the Operator provides them free on request, appropriate to the child's age and
weight, where Guideless asks at booking.

> _Note: C3.3 and C3.4 matter because travelers, sometimes travelling alone, get into a stranger's
> car at night in a foreign country with no Guideless representative present._

## C4. Flight monitoring

C4.1 Guideless gives the flight number and scheduled arrival time when it books.

C4.2 **The Operator tracks the flight** and adjusts the pickup to the actual arrival. A delayed
flight is not a no-show and attracts no extra charge within the waiting time in C5.

C4.3 If a flight is diverted, cancelled or rescheduled to another day, the Operator will contact
Guideless and rebook at no charge where it reasonably can.

C4.4 If the Operator cannot track a flight for any reason, it says so here so Guideless knows not to
rely on it: `[FLIGHT TRACKING LIMITS]`.

## C5. Meeting the traveler, and waiting time

C5.1 The driver meets the traveler `[MEETING ARRANGEMENT — e.g. in the arrivals hall at Terminal 2,
holding a name board]` and is in place by the time the traveler clears customs.

C5.2 Included waiting time, free of charge:

| Pickup type      | Free waiting                              |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Airport arrival  | `[AIRPORT WAIT]` from actual landing time |
| Hotel or address | `[ADDRESS WAIT]` from the booked time     |

C5.3 After that, waiting is charged at `[WAITING RATE]`, in `[INCREMENT]` increments. The Operator
will contact Guideless before the free time runs out, not after.

C5.4 **The driver may not leave without telephoning Guideless on `[24/7 PHONE NUMBER]` and giving
Guideless a reasonable chance to reach the traveler.** A driver who leaves without doing so is not
entitled to the fare.

C5.5 The Operator provides the driver's name, mobile number and vehicle details to Guideless at
least `[DRIVER DETAILS HOURS]` hours before the pickup, so Guideless can pass them to the traveler.

> _Note: C5.4 is the clause that prevents the most likely serious failure — a traveler who lands
> late, clears a slow border, and finds the driver gone with nobody to call._

## C6. Luggage

C6.1 The vehicle class booked must carry the luggage stated in C2.1. The Operator will tell
Guideless at booking if the party's luggage will not fit, rather than discovering it at the kerb.

C6.2 Where a traveler brings more than stated, the Operator will do what it reasonably can, and may
charge `[EXCESS LUGGAGE CHARGE]` if a second vehicle is genuinely needed. It will call Guideless
first.

C6.3 The Operator handles luggage into and out of the vehicle.

C6.4 The Operator is responsible for luggage while it is in its vehicle, to the limits of its
insurance.

## C7. No-shows and cancellation

C7.1 A traveler who does not appear, and cannot be reached under C5.4, is a no-show and the fare is
charged in full.

C7.2 Guideless may cancel free of charge up to `[FREE CANCELLATION HOURS]` hours before the pickup.
After that, `[LATE CANCELLATION CHARGE]`.

C7.3 Where a traveler's flight is cancelled and Guideless tells the Operator as soon as it knows, no
charge applies even inside the window in C7.2.

C7.4 If the Operator fails to appear, or is so late that the traveler must make their own way,
Guideless pays nothing and the Operator reimburses the traveler's reasonable alternative transport.

## C8. Late night and early morning

C8.1 The Operator covers pickups **at any hour, every day, including public holidays**, on the
routes in C2.

C8.2 Where it cannot cover a particular window, it says so here rather than declining on the night:
`[HOURS NOT COVERED]`.

C8.3 The Operator's overnight contact is `[SUPPLIER 24/7 PHONE]`, answered by a person.

## C9. Safety and incidents

C9.1 The Operator reports incidents under clause 14 of the master agreement: **immediately, at any
hour**. This includes a road traffic accident however minor, a traveler taken ill in the vehicle, or
any interaction with police.

C9.2 Drivers do not use a handheld phone while driving and comply with drivers' hours rules.

C9.3 The Operator will not allow a driver to work while impaired by alcohol, drugs or fatigue.

## C10. Insurance

C10.1 In addition to clause 15 of the master agreement, the Operator carries motor and passenger
liability insurance valid for commercial passenger transport, with a minimum limit of `[LIMIT]`, in
every country on the route.

C10.2 The route `[ROUTE]` crosses between France and Monaco. The Operator confirms its licences and
insurance are valid in both.

C10.3 The Operator will provide a certificate naming Guideless before the first booking.

> _Note: C10.2 is specific to the Nice ↔ Monaco route and is easy to overlook. Guideless should also
> ask its own broker about hired and non-owned auto cover, per `docs/business-readiness.md` §2.2._

## C11. Data protection

C11.1 The Operator receives the traveler's name, mobile number, flight number and pickup and drop-off
addresses, and nothing more.

C11.2 The Operator will not use these details to market to the traveler, and will delete them within
`[DATA RETENTION]` after the transfer, except where law or an incident requires otherwise.

C11.3 The Operator acts as a **`[PROCESSOR / CONTROLLER]`** for this data — to be settled with the
attorney under clause 17.3 of the master agreement.
