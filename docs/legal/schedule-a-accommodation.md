## A1. Rooming

A1.1 **Guideless books single occupancy by default.** Every traveler has their own room unless
Guideless's booking says otherwise.

A1.2 Two travelers may share one room. **Guideless never places three travelers in a room**, and the
Property may not propose a triple, a rollaway or a sofa bed as a way to meet an allocation.

A1.3 A shared room must be a genuine twin with two separate beds, unless the booking says the
travelers want a double. The Property will confirm the bed type at booking, not on arrival.

A1.4 Room types and the rate for each:

| Room type       | Occupancy | Bed     | Net rate per room per night | Included  |
| --------------- | --------- | ------- | --------------------------- | --------- |
| `[ROOM TYPE 1]` | 1         | `[BED]` | `[RATE]`                    | `[MEALS]` |
| `[ROOM TYPE 2]` | 2         | Twin    | `[RATE]`                    | `[MEALS]` |
| `[ROOM TYPE 3]` | 2         | Double  | `[RATE]`                    | `[MEALS]` |

A1.5 Rooms of the same type must be of a comparable standard. The Property may not give some of the
group its worst rooms because they were booked together. Where rooms differ materially — a courtyard
view against a sea view, a floor without a lift — the Property must say so at booking so Guideless
can either decline or tell travelers.

> _Note: A1.1 to A1.3 are product facts, not preferences. Guideless prices its trips on single
> occupancy and gives a per-person discount when two share, so a Property that substitutes a triple
> breaks the price the traveler paid._

## A2. Allocation, release and attrition

A2.1 The Property holds the following allocation for Guideless:

| Departure     | Dates                    | Rooms held | Release date     |
| ------------- | ------------------------ | ---------- | ---------------- |
| `[DEPARTURE]` | `[CHECK-IN – CHECK-OUT]` | `[N]`      | `[RELEASE DATE]` |

A2.2 Guideless may release any part of the allocation, without charge, up to the release date.

A2.3 After the release date, unsold rooms return to the Property automatically and Guideless owes
nothing for them.

A2.4 **Attrition:** Guideless may reduce a confirmed group booking by up to `[ATTRITION PERCENT]`%
of rooms without charge until `[ATTRITION DATE]`. Reductions beyond that are charged under A5.

A2.5 The Property will tell Guideless when the allocation is `[LOW WATERMARK]` rooms or fewer, so
Guideless can decide whether to extend it.

## A3. Rates, taxes and what the traveler pays locally

A3.1 Rates in A1.4 are **net to Guideless**, in `[CURRENCY]`, and include VAT and service.

A3.2 Rates are valid for the dates in A2.1. They do not change because the city becomes busy, an
event is announced, or demand rises. This includes Grand Prix week.

A3.3 **The only charge a traveler pays at the Property is the city or tourist tax**, currently
`[CITY TAX AMOUNT]` per person per night. The Property will state it at check-in and issue a
receipt.

A3.4 Nothing else may be charged to a traveler as a condition of the stay: no resort fee, no
mandatory service charge, no compulsory breakfast supplement, no key or towel deposit taken as a
charge rather than a hold.

A3.5 Incidentals a traveler chooses — minibar, room service, laundry, spa, parking — are between the
traveler and the Property. The Property may take a card authorisation on arrival for these.
Guideless does not guarantee them and will not be invoiced for them.

> _Note: A3.3 and A3.4 exist because Guideless publishes a total price and tells travelers exactly
> what they will pay on arrival. An undisclosed mandatory fee is a consumer-law problem for
> Guideless, not just an annoyance._

## A4. Meals

A4.1 Breakfast is `[INCLUDED / NOT INCLUDED]`. Where included, it is `[BUFFET / CONTINENTAL / À LA
CARTE]`, served from `[TIME]` to `[TIME]`.

A4.2 Where breakfast is included and a traveler leaves before it is served — an early train or an
early flight — the Property will provide `[BREAKFAST TO GO / NOTHING]` at no extra cost.

A4.3 The Property will cater for dietary requirements Guideless notifies in advance, including
vegetarian, vegan, coeliac and stated allergies, at no extra charge. Where it genuinely cannot, it
must say so at booking rather than on the day.

> _Note: A4.2 matters on the Nice → Avignon → Paris trip, where travelers take morning trains
> between cities._

## A5. Check-in, check-out and early departure

A5.1 Check-in from `[TIME]`. Check-out by `[TIME]`.

A5.2 The Property will hold luggage free of charge before check-in and after check-out on the day of
departure.

A5.3 Travelers arrive on their own flights and their own schedule. **The Property will accept a
traveler arriving at any hour** and will have a documented way to check in a guest who arrives after
reception closes. If reception is not staffed overnight, the Property must say so here:
`[OVERNIGHT ARRIVAL ARRANGEMENT]`.

A5.4 If a traveler leaves early, the Property charges `[EARLY DEPARTURE TERMS]`. Where the departure
is because of illness, injury or a family emergency, the Property will waive the charge.

> _Note: A5.3 is a product fact. Guideless does not meet travelers at the airport unless they buy a
> transfer, and there is no Guideless representative on site, so a locked door at midnight is a real
> failure mode._

## A6. Accessible rooms and special requirements

A6.1 The Property confirms it has `[N]` accessible rooms, with the following features:
`[FEATURES — step-free access, roll-in shower, grab rails, visual alarm, lift to floor]`.

A6.2 Where Guideless books an accessible room, the Property guarantees it. It may not be reassigned
to another guest, and a "similar" room is not a substitute.

A6.3 The Property will tell Guideless of anything that would affect a traveler with limited
mobility: no lift, stairs to reception, a room reached only by steps, works in progress.

## A7. Cancellation

A7.1 Cancellation charges, per room:

| When Guideless cancels                           | Charge                   |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| More than `[FREE CANCELLATION DAYS]` days before | Nothing                  |
| `[TIER 2 DAYS]` days before                      | `[PERCENT]`% of the stay |
| `[TIER 3 DAYS]` days before                      | `[PERCENT]`% of the stay |
| Inside `[FINAL DAYS]` days                       | `[PERCENT]`% of the stay |

A7.2 The Property will reduce any charge by what it earns reselling the room, and will tell
Guideless if it resells.

A7.3 A no-show is charged as a cancellation inside the final tier. The Property will hold the room
until `[NO-SHOW HOUR]` before releasing it, and will tell Guideless before it does.

A7.4 Where Guideless cancels because a departure did not reach its minimum number of travelers, and
does so before `[MINIMUM VIABILITY DATE]`, no charge applies.

> _Note: A7.4 protects the case where a trip does not fill. Guideless publishes a minimum party size
> per departure and must be able to cancel the whole thing without paying for empty rooms._

## A8. If the Property changes or cannot deliver

A8.1 If the Property cannot house a traveler it has confirmed, clause 10 of the master agreement
applies: an **equivalent or better property at the Property's cost**, in the same area, plus any
transport cost, plus notification to Guideless before the traveler is affected.

A8.2 "Equivalent or better" means at least the same classification, the same room type and
occupancy, breakfast on the same basis, and no further from the traveler's route. A property further
out with a taxi voucher is not equivalent.

A8.3 If the Property is sold, rebranded, closed for renovation, or loses its classification, it must
tell Guideless within `[NOTIFICATION DAYS]` days. Guideless may then cancel affected bookings
without charge.

A8.4 The Property will tell Guideless about building work, a closed pool, a closed restaurant or a
lift out of service that would affect a traveler's stay, as soon as it knows.

A8.5 **Guideless publishes the property name to travelers.** A substitution is therefore visible and
must be agreed with Guideless, not arranged directly with the traveler at the desk.

## A9. Damage

A9.1 A traveler is responsible for damage they cause. The Property deals with that traveler
directly and may charge their card.

A9.2 The Property will tell Guideless about anything beyond ordinary wear before it charges, and
will provide photographs and an itemised cost.

A9.3 Guideless is not responsible for damage caused by a traveler, and will not be invoiced for it.

## A10. Data protection

A10.1 For the guest record it keeps for its own legal, tax and commercial purposes, the Property is
a **controller** in its own right.

A10.2 For the traveler list Guideless sends to make the booking, the Property acts as a
**`[PROCESSOR / CONTROLLER]`** — to be settled with the attorney under clause 17.3 of the master
agreement.

A10.3 The Property may not add Guideless travelers to a marketing list, or pass their details to a
third party, except where the law requires it (for example police registration forms in France).

## A11. Rate and availability data

A11.1 Where the Property is reachable through a booking platform Guideless uses, it will keep its
rates and availability there current for the contracted dates.

A11.2 Where a rate returned by a platform is lower than the rate in A1.4, Guideless may take the
lower rate.

A11.3 A rate the Property publishes is binding for `[RATE VALIDITY HOURS]` hours from the time it
is returned to Guideless.

> _Note: A11 exists because Guideless re-checks the live rate before it charges a traveler and
> refuses the sale if it has moved more than 5%. A binding validity window is what makes that
> re-check meaningful rather than advisory._
